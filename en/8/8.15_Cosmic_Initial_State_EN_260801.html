7.2　Key Finding: Statistical Correlation between Cycling and Ground State

Spectral computation reveals a statistical trend: at the single-particle level, there is a correlation between “three-world cycling” and “\(S \approx 137\) ground state,” but it is not absolute mutual exclusion.

  1. Among regions whose \(S\) value is close to the electron ground state \(S_e = 137\) (\(|S - S_e| < 10\), 14 regions total), 11 regions have \(H_{ij}\) frequency ratios tending to \(1.0\) (trivial), with continued fraction approximation determined as \(m = 1, n = 1\), excluded from single-particle cycling. This statistical correlation indicates: single-particle three-world cycling is more likely to occur in angular configurations deviating from the ground state, but a few ground-state-type configurations can also cycle. Complex systems of the material world (such as living organisms) can only emerge from ground-state particles through multi-body combination; their mechanism is discussed elsewhere.
