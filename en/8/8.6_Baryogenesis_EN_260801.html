§1 Geometric Realization of the Sakharov Conditions

1.1 The Three Sakharov Conditions

The generation of matter-antimatter asymmetry requires three conditions (Sakharov, 1967):

  1. Baryon number violation: \(B\) is not conserved
  2. C and CP violation: differential production rate of matter versus antimatter
  3. Non-equilibrium state: CPT ensures net \(B = 0\) under thermal equilibrium

In the Standard Model, the degree of satisfaction of these three conditions is insufficient — additional mechanisms (such as leptogenesis) are required. In Conjugate Spectral Geometry, the three conditions are automatically satisfied by the algebraic structure.

1.3 Insufficiency of Standard Model Baryogenesis

Difficulties of Standard Model electroweak baryogenesis:

  • Insufficient CP violation: The CP-violating magnitude of the CKM phase is \(\sim 10^{-20}\), far smaller than the observed \(\eta_B \sim 10^{-10}\)
  • Insufficient phase transition strength: The electroweak phase transition at \(m_H \approx 125\) GeV is not a first-order transition and cannot produce sufficient non-equilibrium
  • Leptogenesis required: Standard Model baryogenesis is insufficient, requiring right-handed-neutrino-driven leptogenesis

Conjugate Spectral Geometry resolves these three difficulties from the algebraic structure — CP violation comes from triality breaking (not CKM), the non-equilibrium state comes from the 𝒞-sector time arrow (not phase transition), and baryon number violation comes from ℳ-sector mass generation (not instantons).

\[\eta_B = \frac{n_B - n_{\bar{B}}}{n_\gamma} = \frac{\sigma_\mathcal{C}^*}{\sigma_\mathcal{M}^*} \cdot \delta_{\text{CP}} \cdot \eta_{\text{geom}} \cdot \mathcal{A}_{\text{NE}},\]

Proof.

Step 1: \(Z_3\) after triality breaking (Theorem 4.5.1.01). Among the six elements of \(S_3\), the identity and the two 3-cycles (123) and (132) are retained in \(Z_3\), while the three transpositions are eliminated by projection. This selection produces the CP asymmetry parameter:

\[\delta_{\text{CP}} = \frac{\gamma - (-\gamma)}{\gamma(-\gamma)} = \frac{4\pi}{-4\pi^2} = -\frac{1}{\pi} \approx -0.318.\]

But the effective CP asymmetry is diluted by sector weights:

\[\delta_{\text{CP}}^{\text{eff}} = \frac{\sigma_\mathcal{I}^*}{\sigma_\mathcal{M}^*} \approx 0.014.\]

Algebraic derivation of CP asymmetry. The irreducible representations of the \(S_3\) group: identity (\(A_1\)), sign (\(A_2\)), two-dimensional (\(E\)). The triality breaking \(S_3 \to Z_2\) projects \(A_2\) and \(E\):

  • \(A_2\) → \(A\) (trivial representation of \(Z_3\), 3-cycles are even permutations)
