The triggering mechanism is determined by the residual encoding recombination rate falling below the critical value (Theorem 4.1). The current residual encoding is intact ($\varepsilon_{\text{res}} \approx 1$), and the system is stable. The specific triggering time of the disintegration stage cannot be independently predicted from intrinsic constants and is an open problem.
