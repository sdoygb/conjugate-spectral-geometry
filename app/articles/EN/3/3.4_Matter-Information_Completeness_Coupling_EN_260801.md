### 3.1 Concept of Information Back-Reaction

1. The coding $\sigma_\mathcal{M}$ of the Matter Sector is transmitted to the Information Sector via $P_{\sigma_2}$

3. The changed $\sigma_\mathcal{I}$ is transmitted back to the Matter Sector via $P_{\sigma_2}^{-1} = P_{\sigma_2}$

This is a "closed-loop" process——matter influences information, and information reacts back on matter.
