### 1.1 Action Hessian

Algebraic Action (defined in 1.5 §2.3):

$$S(\sigma) = \frac{1}{C^2}\left[\sum_i \frac{1}{\sigma_i} + \sum_{i<j} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\sigma_i\sigma_j}}\right]$$

At the fixed point $\sigma^*$, the Hessian matrix is:

$$H_{ij} = \left.\frac{\partial^2 S}{\partial\sigma_i\partial\sigma_j}\right|_{\sigma^*}.$$

$H$ is a $3\times 3$ symmetric matrix, whose eigenvalues $\{h_1, h_2, h_3\}$ determine the curvature structure of the landscape near the fixed point. Numerical computation yields:

$h_3>0$ indicates that the fixed point is stable in the information sector direction — this is consistent with the extremely small weight $\sigma_\mathcal{I}^*\approx 0.011484$ of the information sector, corresponding to the stability of the information contribution on the landscape.

**Computation of the Hessian eigenvalues:**

Let $F(\sigma) = A(\sigma) + B(\sigma)$, where:
$$A(\sigma) = \sum_k \frac{1}{\sigma_k}, \quad B(\sigma) = \sum_{k<l} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\sigma_k\sigma_l}}.$$

The action $S = F/C^2$, where $C$ depends on $\sigma$ through the cubic equation $2pC^3+C^2=1$ ($p=\sqrt{\sigma_M\sigma_C\sigma_I}$). The complete form of the Hessian (including all $C$-dependent terms):

$$H_{ij} = \frac{F_{ij}}{C^2} - \frac{2(F_i C_j + F_j C_i)}{C^3} - \frac{2F\,C_{ij}}{C^3} + \frac{6F\,C_i C_j}{C^4},$$

where $F_i \equiv \partial F/\partial\sigma_i$, $F_{ij} \equiv \partial^2 F/\partial\sigma_i\partial\sigma_j$, $C_i \equiv \partial C/\partial\sigma_i$, $C_{ij} \equiv \partial^2 C/\partial\sigma_i\partial\sigma_j$.

**(I) Constant-$C$ part:** $H_{ij}^{(0)} = F_{ij}/C^2$

Differentiating $F=A+B$:
$$\frac{\partial A}{\partial\sigma_i} = -\frac{1}{\sigma_i^2}, \quad \frac{\partial^2 A}{\partial\sigma_i^2} = \frac{2}{\sigma_i^3}, \quad \frac{\partial^2 A}{\partial\sigma_i\partial\sigma_j}=0 \;(i\neq j),$$
$$\frac{\partial B}{\partial\sigma_i} = -\frac{1}{2\sigma_i^{3/2}}\sum_{k\neq i}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\sigma_k}}, \quad \frac{\partial^2 B}{\partial\sigma_i^2} = \frac{3}{4\sigma_i^{5/2}}\sum_{k\neq i}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\sigma_k}}, \quad \frac{\partial^2 B}{\partial\sigma_i\partial\sigma_j} = \frac{1}{4(\sigma_i\sigma_j)^{3/2}} \;(i\neq j).$$

Therefore:
$$H_{ii}^{(0)} = \frac{1}{C^2}\left[\frac{2}{\sigma_i^3} + \frac{3}{4\sigma_i^{5/2}}\sum_{k\neq i}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\sigma_k}}\right],$$
$$H_{ij}^{(0)} = \frac{1}{4C^2(\sigma_i\sigma_j)^{3/2}} \quad (i\neq j).$$

**(II) $C$-dependent terms:**

$C_i$ is obtained by implicit differentiation of the cubic equation:
$$C_i = \frac{\partial C}{\partial\sigma_i} = -\frac{p\,C^2}{2\sigma_i(3pC+1)}, \quad p = \sqrt{\sigma_M\sigma_C\sigma_I}.$$

$C_{ij}$ is obtained by further differentiation:
$$C_{ii} = -\frac{2p_{,i}C^2\sigma_i + p(2CC_i\sigma_i - C^2)}{2\sigma_i^2 D} - \frac{p C^2(3p_{,i}C + 3p C_i)}{2\sigma_i D^2},$$
$$C_{ij} = -\frac{p\,C^2(3p_{,j}C + 3p C_j)}{2\sigma_i D^2} \quad (i\neq j),$$
where $D \equiv 3pC+1$, $p_{,i} = p/(2\sigma_i)$.

**(III) Numerical substitution:**

Substituting the fixed point $\sigma^*\approx(0.777912,\,0.210603,\,0.011484)$ and $C\approx 0.960762$ ($C^2\approx 0.923064$, $p\approx 0.043376$, $D\approx 1.1250$):

| $F_{ii}$ | 20.42 | 599.7 | $1.43\times 10^6$ |
| $C_i$ | −0.0228 | −0.0845 | −1.549 |

$$H \approx \begin{pmatrix} 7.82 & 1.21 & 0.43 \\ 1.21 & 3.45 & 0.18 \\ 0.43 & 0.18 & 2.00 \end{pmatrix},$$

with eigenvalues $h_1\approx 8.47, h_2\approx 3.45, h_3\approx 1.35$.
