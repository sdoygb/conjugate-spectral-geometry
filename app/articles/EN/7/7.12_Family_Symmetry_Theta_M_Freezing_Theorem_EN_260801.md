**Root cause of the $52\%$ deviation**: the formula $a_X \propto \langle Q \rangle_X / \cos\theta_M^0$ of the anomalous magnetic moment article was derived under the premise of $\eta=0$ paths ($\theta_M$ fixed = $\theta_M^0$). In Scheme B, $\theta_M$ varies from $57.93^\circ$ to $31.94^\circ$ — the $A_\eta$ component of the Berry connection is no longer zero, and the mathematical structure of the path average needs to be generalized.

**This does not constitute a refutation of G2.** It merely indicates that the Berry phase formulation of the anomalous magnetic moment article is a specialized version for Scheme A — Scheme B requires a more general framework.

### §6.6　Mass Spectrum Self-Consistency Cross-Check

Before proceeding with the Berry phase generalization discussion, a crucial self-consistency check is performed. Mass operator M̂ (Theorem 1.1.5.01-20): $m = K \sin^3\theta_M$, $K = 839.758793$ keV.

**Scheme B**: $\theta_M^\mu = 31.94^\circ$, $\sin^3 31.94^\circ \approx 0.1483$.

$$m_\mu^{\text{B}} = 839.759 \times 0.1483 \approx 124.5\ \text{keV}$$

Experimental value: $m_\mu = 105.66\ \text{MeV} = 105660\ \text{keV}$. Deviation factor: **$\times 849$**.

**Scheme A** (control): $\theta_M^\mu = 57.93^\circ$, $\sin^3 57.93^\circ \approx 0.6086$.

$$m_\mu^{\text{A}} = 839.759 \times 0.6086 \approx 511\ \text{keV}$$

Deviation factor: $\times 207$.

**Diagnosis**: In both schemes, the simple form $m = K\sin^3\theta_M$ of the mass operator M̂ cannot directly give the correct muon mass. Scheme B's deviation is larger ($849$ vs $207$), but this is a quantitative difference, not a qualitative one.

This means: for excited generations (μ and τ), the mass formula needs to include additional enhancement factors — possibly from the contribution of $S$ (the Algebraic Action): $m_{\text{eff}} = K\sin^3\theta_M \cdot f(S)$, or from the contribution of the displacement $\Delta\xi$, or from the feedback of the Berry phase itself.

**This is not a problem unique to Scheme B — Scheme A has the same qualitative problem.** The problem was not emphasized in the anomalous magnetic moment article (which focused on magnetic moments rather than masses), but it is an honest open problem that any three-generation lepton geometric model must face. We file it as **G2b**.

### §6.7　Necessity of Generalizing the Berry Phase Formulation

The path average formulas of §7–§8 of the anomalous magnetic moment article are built on the following premises:
