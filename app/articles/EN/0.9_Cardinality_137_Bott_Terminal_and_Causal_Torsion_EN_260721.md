### 5.3 Mathematical Mechanism of Leakage: From Irreducible Terms Inside Zero-Sum to External Leakage

### 5.4 Why Addition?——Non-Commutativity of Container and Leakage

Since they come from different zero-sum levels and measure different dimensions of the coding space (static capacity vs. dynamical residue), they must combine by addition.

**Proof by contradiction**: If 128 and 9 multiplied, we would get $128 \times 9 = 1152$——this is neither 137, nor does it agree at all with the purely algebraic computation $S(\sigma^*) \approx 137.036$ from the fixed point $\sigma^*$ in 2.2. **Independent verification**: The integer part of $S(\sigma^*)$ is exactly 137, consistent with $128+9$. This is not a coincidence——$S(\sigma^*)$ is computed purely algebraically from the fixed-point equation of the Coding Orbit, completely independent of the "container + leakage" geometric argument. The agreement of the two is independent verification.
