### 3.1 Double Stochasticity Guarantee

The double stochasticity of $T$ (row-sum = column-sum = 1) guarantees the self-consistency of the three-field coupling:

$$\sum_j T_{ij} = 1 \implies \text{total output of each sector} = \text{total input}.$$

The deeper meaning of double stochasticity is the **coding conservation law**. View the three-field coupling as a flow network of coding information: the coding information of the Matter Sector ℳ flows to 𝒞 via $P_\tau$ (weak interaction channel) and to ℐ via $P_{\sigma_2}$ (gravitational channel), while simultaneously receiving backflow from 𝒞 and ℐ. The algebraic formulation of this conservation law is: at any time $t$, the sum of the three-field coding weights is identically 1:

$$\sigma_{\mathcal{M}}(t) + \sigma_{\mathcal{C}}(t) + \sigma_{\mathcal{I}}(t) = 1, \quad \forall t.$$

This is the dynamical corollary of the normalization condition—double stochasticity guarantees that normalization is preserved under time evolution.

Algebraic closure of the three-field coupling means: all matrix entries of the three-field coupling matrix $M_5$ are uniquely determined by a finite set of algebraic parameters ($\theta_I, \theta_\tau, \theta_{\sigma_2}, \chi_M, \chi_C, \chi_I$), and these parameters are uniquely locked by Bott periodicity, triality breaking, and the Birkhoff fixed point—there are no free parameters.

- **Testability of predictions**: closure guarantees that predictions are definite—there are no adjustable parameters to "fit" experiment.

### 5.2 Closure and Cross-Region Information Import

Algebraic closure is the mathematical guarantee of cross-region information import. Closure ensures:

1. **Physical constants are not free parameters**—they are uniquely determined by algebraic structure.
2. **The precision of physical constants is limited only by computational accuracy**—there are no "unknown" parameters.
3. **Cross-region information import is bidirectional**—from pure geometry to physics (e.g., from Bott periodicity to $\alpha^{-1}$) and from physics feeding back to geometry (e.g., from $\alpha^{-1} \approx 137$ inferring $\chi$) are both definite.

### 5.3 Falsifiability of Closure

Algebraic closure is falsifiable. If any of the following situations is discovered, the algebraic closure of the three-field coupling is falsified:

1. **Existence of independently adjustable free parameters**: if experiment requires introducing a free parameter not present in the algebraic structure to match observation, closure is broken.
2. **Parameter inconsistency**: if the same parameter derived from different physical constants is inconsistent (e.g., $\theta_I$ derived from $\alpha$ and from $\sin^2\theta_W$ differ), closure is broken.
3. **Irreconcilable discrepancy between prediction and experiment**: if the predictions given by closure have ineliminable deviations from experiment (beyond running corrections and observational errors), closure is falsified.

The falsifiability of algebraic closure makes the three-field coupling theory a scientific theory—it provides definite, testable predictions, not unfalsifiable philosophical statements.
