Starting from the {2,3,5} Interlock Property framework, we study the stable configurations of two particles on the joint Sector Parameter Space and the geometric origin of the quarkonium spectrum. The theoretical framework establishes correspondences with the inevitable structure of the quarkonium spectrum: Dirac spectral energy scale → rigid locking of deeply bound states; many-body spectral parallelism → tetraquark families; metastable saddle-point solutions → non-color-singlet narrow resonance spectra.

§2.1 Joint Sector Parameter Space

Let \(p_1, p_2 \in \Sigma_\eta\) be two minima of the Algebraic Action. The joint Sector Parameter Space \(\Sigma_{\text{joint}}^{(2)}\) is defined as the set of points in the product \((\Sigma_\eta)^2\) of two copies of \(\Sigma_\eta\) satisfying the ℳ Sector synchronization constraint \(\theta_M^{(1)} = \theta_M^{(2)}\).

Theorem 3.8.2.01 (Existence): Suppose the geodesic distance between two Algebraic Action minima in the ℳ Sector is less than \(\pi / \sqrt{\lambda_1^{\text{eff}}}\). Then \(\Sigma_{\text{joint}}^{(2)}\) is a non-empty smooth submanifold of \((\Sigma_\eta)^2\) of dimension 3.

Proof: The product space of two copies has dimension 4. The ℳ Sector synchronization constraint imposes 1 independent equation. By the Transversality Theorem, when the ℳ coordinates of the two particles are sufficiently close (less than the Spectral Gap characteristic scale \(\pi / \sqrt{\lambda_1^{\text{eff}}}\)), the gradients of the constraint functions are linearly independent, and the intersection is a smooth submanifold of codimension 1 and dimension 3. From the Sector Parameter Constraint \(\theta_M + \theta_C + \theta_I = 90^\circ\), after synchronizing \(\theta_M\), the two particles share \(\theta_C^{(i)} + \theta_I^{(i)} = 90^\circ - \theta_M\) as a common constant; each particle retains 1 degree of freedom in the \((\theta_C, \theta_I)\) plane, so the joint Sector Parameter Space consists of a 1-dimensional shared ℳ coordinate and 2 independent phase degrees of freedom (1 degree of freedom per particle on the \((\theta_C, \theta_I)\) constraint plane). Since \(\Sigma_\eta\) is a compact manifold, so is \((\Sigma_\eta)^2\); thus a non-empty smooth submanifold \(\Sigma_{\text{joint}}^{(2)}\) satisfying the distance condition must exist.

Furthermore, constrained by the Coding Orbit discrete approximation layer, the information unit count of the joint Sector Parameter Space is bounded by \(N_{\text{info}}^{(2)} = S_e^2 \Lambda^{2n} C_{\text{joint}}\), where \(C_{\text{joint}}\) is the joint Sector Parameter Space geometric factor and \(n \in [0, 7]\) is the hierarchy index. \(\square\)
