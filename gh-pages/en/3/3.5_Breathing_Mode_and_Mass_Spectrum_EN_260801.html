Proof. The Coding Orbit is divided into three segments (Matter, Causal, Information), each contributing one dimension on the Birkhoff Polytope. §2 Breathing Mode Frequency Formula

  • \(\lambda_{2,3} = 0.678 \pm 0.181i\) (\(|\lambda_{2,3}| = 0.702 < 1\))

\[\det(T - \lambda I) = -\lambda^3 + \text{tr}(T)\lambda^2 - \frac{1}{2}[\text{tr}(T)^2 - \text{tr}(T^2)]\lambda + \det(T).\]

Substituting the concrete matrix elements of \(T = \theta_I I + \theta_\tau P_\tau + \theta_{\sigma_2} P_{\sigma_2}\) and solving the cubic equation yields the three Eigenvalues above.

2.2 Breathing Frequency Formula

Proposition 3.5.2.01 (Breathing Frequency Formula). The frequency of the Matter Sector breathing mode is determined by the imaginary part of the non-dominant Eigenvalue of the Birkhoff Mixing Matrix:

\[\omega_{\text{breath}} = \frac{|\text{Im}(\lambda_2)|}{2\pi} = \frac{0.181}{2\pi} \approx 0.029.\]

The angular frequency of the breathing is:

\[\Omega_{\text{breath}} = |\text{Im}(\lambda_2)| = 0.181.\]

The period of the breathing is:

\[T_{\text{breath}} = \frac{2\pi}{\Omega_{\text{breath}}} = \frac{2\pi}{0.181} \approx 34.7.\]

In units of coding steps, the breathing period is approximately 35 steps.

2.3 Breathing Decay Time

\[\tau_{\text{breath}} = \frac{1}{|\ln|\lambda_2||} = \frac{1}{|\ln 0.702|} \approx 2.82.\]
