1.1 Algebraic Action

The Algebraic Action of Conjugate Spectral Geometry is:

\[S(\sigma) = \frac{1}{C^2}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{1}{\sigma_i} + \sum_{i<j}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\sigma_i\sigma_j}}\right], \quad \kappa=1.\]

Geometric significance of the action:

The two terms of the Algebraic Action \(S(\sigma)\) correspond to two types of geometric contributions:

  1. Diagonal term \(\sum_i 1/\sigma_i\): the “self-energy” contribution of the three sectors — the reciprocal of each sector weight measures the energy density of that sector. The smaller \(\sigma_i\), the larger the energy contribution of that sector.

  2. Cross term \(\sum_{i<j} 1/\sqrt{\sigma_i\sigma_j}\): the “coupling” contribution among the three sectors — the reciprocal of the geometric mean of sector weights measures the coupling strength between sectors. The geometric mean form of this term comes from the Coding Weight amplitude relation \(\Delta\lambda_i\propto\sqrt{\sigma_i}\) — the inter-sector coupling is proportional to the reciprocal of the product of amplitudes.

  3. Normalization factor \(1/C^2\): determined by the Algebraic Normalization \(2pC^3 + C^2=1\), \(C\) measures the overall balance of the three sector weights. The larger \(C\) (closer to 1), the more balanced the sectors; the smaller \(C\), the more imbalanced the sectors.
