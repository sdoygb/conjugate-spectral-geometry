Proposition 10.8.3.01 (Geometric Reynolds Number): The geometric Reynolds number Re_geo is defined as the ratio of the effective eigenvalues of the Dirac spectral direction to the spectral gap direction of the Birkhoff Hessian:

\[\text{Re}_{\text{geo}} = \frac{\lambda_2^{\text{eff}}}{\lambda_1^{\text{eff}}} = \frac{59324.3}{391.05} \approx 151.7 \approx 152\]

Mechanical interpretation: In geometric units, the characteristic velocity scale is u₀ = √λ₁^eff (spectral gap direction propagation velocity), and the characteristic length scale is L₀ = 1/√λ₁^eff (spectral gap direction wavelength). Inertial term scale: |(𝐮·∇)𝐮| ∼ u₀²/L₀ = (λ₁eff)(3/2). Viscous term scale: |ν_geo ∇²𝐮| ∼ (1/λ₂^eff)·u₀/L₀² = (λ₁eff)(3/2)/λ₂^eff. Hence:

\[\frac{\text{Inertia}}{\text{Viscosity}} = \frac{\lambda_2^{\text{eff}}}{\lambda_1^{\text{eff}}} = \text{Re}_{\text{geo}}\]

Derivation basis: ν_geo = 1/λ₂^eff (§2.3 construction), u₀ = √λ₁^eff (Theorem 7.8.3.01 Lie group generator), L₀ = 1/√λ₁^eff (Proposition 2.2 step size).
